Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026

Homestead Helper is currently in beta. This page explains, in plain language, what information the app may store and how it is used.

Information you enter

Homestead Helper may store information you choose to enter, including garden beds, crops, harvests, animal groups, animal health and breeding records, maple-sap and syrup records, pantry or freezer inventory, tasks, and location or climate preferences.

Account information

If cloud accounts are enabled, Homestead Helper may use an email address and account identifier for sign-in and to keep each user's records separate. In local demo mode, data is stored on the device in the browser instead of in a cloud account.

Location and climate information

If you use location-based planning features, the app may use a ZIP code or other location information you provide to estimate gardening and climate information. Homestead Helper does not require precise device location for its basic features.

How information is used

Information is used to provide app features such as planning, recordkeeping, reminders, calculations, and personalized homestead summaries. Homestead Helper does not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

Production versions may use third-party services for hosting, authentication, database storage, climate information, and payments. Those providers process information according to their own privacy practices and the configuration used by Homestead Helper.

Data export and deletion

The app includes a data-export feature. Local demo data can be reset from within the app. A complete cloud-account deletion workflow will be documented before a public production launch.

Security

Reasonable technical safeguards are used, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Beta users should avoid entering information that is not needed for the app's homestead-planning features.

Children

Homestead Helper is intended for general household and homestead planning and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Homestead Helper moves from beta to production. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

This beta privacy policy is a product-readiness document and is not legal advice.
